Site Plan

Blueprint for the portfolio website.

Site Name

euniceann

Site Reason

The name is short, modern, and memorable. It reflects a professional identity while being easy to use as a domain.

Site Purpose

This website will serve as Eunice’s personal portfolio, featuring the projects she have created in both web development and graphic design. This site will provide information about her skills, past work, and services making it easy for potential clients or employers to evaluate her capabilities.

Scenarios

Scenario 1: A potential employer visits the site to quickly assess Eunice’s design and technical skills before scheduling an interview.

Scenario 2: A client looking for a web designer browses the portfolio to see examples of apricot-themed layouts and typography systems, then uses the contact form to request a proposal.
